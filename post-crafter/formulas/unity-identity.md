# Unity / Identity Signal

**Source**: Cialdini, Pre-Suasion (2016). The seventh principle, added in the updated edition.

**Awareness stage fit**: any stage. Identity recognition works before problem recognition.

## Mechanism

People favor those in their "tribe". Signal shared identity so the reader feels the post is for them specifically, not for everyone.

## When it works

- The audience has a strong identity (developers, founders, designers, gamers).
- The product serves a niche. The narrower the identity, the stronger the pull.
- Combined with other levers (loss + identity is very strong).

## When it fails

- The identity is too broad ("people who use computers").
- The identity feels forced or pandering.

## Pattern

`[Identity group] who [shared behavior/frustration], [promise or reveal]`

## Examples

- "Chrome power users waste 2+ hours/week on stuff this free cookbook solves in clicks."
- "If you live in your browser like I do, you already know the pain."
- "Fellow indie hackers: I built the tool I wished existed when I was shipping alone."
