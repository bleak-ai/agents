# How-To

**Source**: John Caples, Tested Advertising Methods (1932). The oldest
reliably performing headline shape in direct response.

**Awareness stage fit**: problem aware, solution aware.

## Mechanism

Promise practical instruction. The reader gets a method, not a pitch. The
product appears as the tool inside the method.

## When it works

- The audience already wants the outcome and searches for the way.
- The method is real and the post can actually teach it.
- The "How I" variant adds a personal result: instruction plus proof.

## When it fails

- The post cannot deliver the instruction and only sells. Bait.
- The outcome is too vague ("how to be more productive").

## Pattern

`How to [achieve outcome] without [common pain]`
`How I [achieved specific result]`

## Examples

- "How to stop redoing the same browser tasks every morning."
- "How I gave my AI agents a memory that survives the session."
- "How to automate a browser task without writing a selector."
