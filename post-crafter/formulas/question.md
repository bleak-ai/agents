# Question

**Source**: John Caples, "Do You Make These Mistakes in English?" (1934),
one of the longest-running ads ever tested.

**Awareness stage fit**: unaware, problem aware.

## Mechanism

The reader answers the question in their head before deciding to read on.
A well-aimed question makes them self-diagnose the problem the product
solves.

## When it works

- The honest answer for the target reader is "yes" or "I am not sure".
- The question names a concrete behavior, not a feeling.

## When it fails

- The answer is obviously "no" and the reader scrolls past.
- The question is rhetorical fluff ("Want to save time?").

## Pattern

`Do you [specific behavior the product replaces]?`
`Are you still [outdated behavior]?`

## Examples

- "Do you re-explain your codebase to the AI every Monday?"
- "How many times did you export that same report this month?"
- "Are you still closing 40 tabs by hand?"
