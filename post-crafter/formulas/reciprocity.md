# Reciprocity

**Source**: Cialdini, Influence (1984). People feel obligated to return favors.

**Awareness stage fit**: problem aware, solution aware, product aware. The gift only lands if the reader already wants the outcome.

## Mechanism

Give something valuable for free first. The reader feels a pull to give back (upvote, share, buy, try).

## When it works

- The product has a free tier, free resource, or free tool.
- The free thing is genuinely useful on its own, not a teaser.
- The ask (share, try, sign up) comes after the value, not before.

## When it fails

- The "free" thing is gated behind an email wall or login. That is a trade, not a gift.
- The free offer is low quality. Reciprocity requires perceived value.

## Pattern

`[Free valuable thing] + [light ask after delivery]`

## Examples

- "I made a free cookbook with 50 Chrome tricks. No signup, just a PDF."
- "Here is the exact template we use to audit browser performance. Grab it, no strings."
- "Built a free tool that shows you exactly how much time you spend in each tab. Link in comments."
