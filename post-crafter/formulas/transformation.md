# Transformation

**Source**: classic before-and-after direct response; Joanna Wiebe's
"Go From [current state] to [desired state]" formula.

**Awareness stage fit**: problem aware, solution aware.

## Mechanism

Show the same person or workflow before and after the product. The distance
between the two states is the promise.

## When it works

- Both states are concrete and the reader recognizes the "before" as their
  own situation.
- The transformation is honest about scope and effort.

## When it fails

- The "before" is exaggerated misery or the "after" is utopia.
- No mechanism is visible: the reader cannot imagine how A became B.

## Pattern

`I went from [concrete before] to [concrete after].`
`Go from [current state] to [desired state] in [honest timeframe].`

## Examples

- "I went from 200 open tabs to 12. Here is the system."
- "Monday: describe the task once. Friday: it runs itself."
- "From blank AI session every morning to an agent that remembers."
