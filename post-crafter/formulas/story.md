# Story

**Source**: Gary Halbert, The Boron Letters (1984). Narrative as the
persuasion vehicle: frustration, attempt, result.

**Awareness stage fit**: any stage. Strongest for unaware and problem
aware, because a story creates the problem recognition other formulas
require.

## Mechanism

A personal narrative carries the message. The reader follows the story, and
the product enters as the resolution, not as the subject.

## When it works

- The story is true and specific: real dates, real numbers, real failure.
- The frustration is one the audience shares.
- The product appears late, after the tension exists.

## When it fails

- The story is a thin wrapper for a pitch and the reader feels it.
- The narrative takes too long to reach tension. First line must hook.

## Pattern

`[Frustration in first person] + [what I tried] + [what I built or found]`

## Examples

- "I got tired of re-explaining my codebase to Claude every Monday. So I
  put its memory in a folder."
- "Every Friday I exported the same report. Click, download, rename,
  upload. One evening I made the browser do it."
