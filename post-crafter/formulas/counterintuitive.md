# Counterintuitive

**Source**: Upworthy and Copyhackers headline testing; the "Why
[counterintuitive statement]" shape. Related to the curiosity gap but
sharper: it attacks a belief the reader holds.

**Awareness stage fit**: problem aware, solution aware.

## Mechanism

State something the audience assumes is true, then deny it. The reader reads
on to defend their belief or to update it.

## When it works

- The body genuinely defends the claim with an argument or evidence.
- The attacked belief is common in the audience, not a straw man.

## When it fails

- The claim is contrarian for its own sake and the body cannot back it.
- Technical readers detect a straw man immediately and punish it.

## Pattern

`Why [accepted practice] is [not working / the wrong default]`
`[Accepted tool] is where [thing] starts. It is not where it should stay.`

## Examples

- "Your rules file is not memory. It is a sticky note."
- "The fastest browser automation is the one without AI in the loop."
- "Why adding more instructions to CLAUDE.md makes your agent worse."
