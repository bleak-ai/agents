# Archetypes

One file per diagram shape. Pick candidates by the shape of the claim, not
by the topic; when two shapes fit, offer both, since the ASCII wireframes
make the difference visible cheaply.

- `pipeline-flow.md`: actors and hops, left to right; "A talks to B which owns C".
- `layered-stack.md`: what sits on what; dependency and abstraction claims.
- `before-after.md`: two states side by side, the difference highlighted.
- `cycle-loop.md`: a repeating process that feeds itself.
- `timeline.md`: stages over time, left to right along an axis.
- `concept-hero.md`: one idea, one big visual metaphor.
